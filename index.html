Version 1.1 (2026-11-13). Method: heat balance for a bare conductor in still indoor air (natural convection and radiation as in CIGRE TB 601, no sun, no wind), AC resistance with skin effect (IEC 60287-1-1). Loop inductance from the partial inductances of all straight segments (Grover). Heat-loss correction 0.93 (calibrated on 95 mm² Al, 355 A at 125 °C). Heat-up time is for the conductor itself; connectors under test follow a few minutes later. Connections: 20 µΩ per joint, 3 per leg (test connector and both leg ends). 1:80 and 1:140 transformers max 230 V, 1:40 max 400 V. 50 Hz.
